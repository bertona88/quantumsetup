How the endless generator works

A deterministic seed creates a new tonal center, mode, motif, drum lattice, and timbral character. At phrase boundaries the engine mutates a few constrained details; every thirty-two bars it creates a new scene. Audio events are scheduled against the hardware audio clock, and every temporary sound disconnects after its envelope, so elapsed runtime does not grow the audio graph. Start with your device volume low.
