# Quantum Contour Contract

Applies to: Infinite Techno `2.1.0`
Scientific status: artistic audio visualization, not a quantum model
Release boundary: deployed `2.1.0` performance release; no scientific claim follows
from deployment

## Boundary

The canvas is an audio-reactive spectral contour. It reads frequency magnitudes from
a Web Audio `AnalyserNode` and combines them with deterministic trigonometric curves,
musical pulse events, and visual envelopes.

Terms such as contour, phase, probability, observation, field, gravity, tunneling,
and coherence are artistic language around the music.

## Nonclaims

The implementation does not:

- solve the Schrödinger equation;
- represent a wavefunction, Hamiltonian, quantum state, measurement, or uncertainty;
- use quantum hardware or quantum randomness;
- map a physical quantum observable to sound;
- establish a scientific Setup Universe interface.

The footer must retain the direct statement that the contour is an artistic metaphor,
not a physics simulation.

## Audio authority

The audio clock and musical planner drive visual pulse state. The visual render loop
never schedules or controls notes. If Canvas 2D is unavailable, audio can continue
with a readable status.

Derived form labels, section run lengths, the 192-bar observation index, material
gesture, candidate score, and lane-clock summaries may inform display or diagnostics
only. The visual layer cannot select a material candidate, mutate or reset a clock,
fulfil a call/answer obligation, or cause tonal, arrangement, bass, or synthesis
changes.

This boundary lets the product have fun with real physics vocabulary without
confusing atmosphere with evidence.
